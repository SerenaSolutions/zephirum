# ZYQL Brand

The LANGUAGE is **ZYQL** (official spelling, always in technical and
documentation use). The name **Lichia Zyko** refers to the RETIRED
emblem/avatar (lychee with gold ⟨Z⟩), superseded by the CEO decision of
2026-10-11.

- Official symbol (CEO, 2026-10-11): the **betta fish** — ascending,
  tilted ~20° diagonal, head slightly above the Z of the wordmark,
  in vivid phosphorescent cyan over deep indigo (#0F1035).
  Meaning: the fish that reaches every corner of the water — the
  adapters that swim between backends; β, the parameter of quantum
  circuits. Art: `assets/brand/logo_zyql_betta_v5_ciano.png`.
- Usage: the fish ALWAYS comes before the wordmark ZYQL, at
  programming-language standard size (Python/Rust mascot scale).
- System glyph (16×16, favicon, terminal, bootloader): ⟨⊢⟩ — the Proof
  Gate. Institutional symbol of the gate: ⟨|⟩ (Dirac).
- Palette: deep indigo #0F1035, white, amber/limão (receipt family).
- Naming precedent: Strawberry Fields (Xanadu) — fruit + quantum;
  inspiration only. All ZYQL marks are original and MIT-covered.
- History: emblem v1 (lychee "Lichia Zyko", navy/violet/gold, CEO
  2026-10-10) retired 2026-10-11 in favor of the betta (v5).
